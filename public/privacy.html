PRIVACY POLICY

Dark Asylum — Protocol 7 · Last updated: 15 September 2026

Summary: the game collects no personal data, stores nothing on a server and shares nothing with anyone. All game data stays on your device.

1. Data we collect

Dark Asylum collects no personal information. The game never asks for your name, email, phone number or location, and there is no sign-up or account of any kind.

2. Data stored on your device

The game saves the following settings in your browser's local storage only:

  • look sensitivity;
  • graphics quality level;
  • sound on/off state;
  • your best completion time.

This data is never transmitted, never shared with third parties, and is erased when you clear your browser data.

3. Network access

The game reaches the network only to download its own files (game code, fonts, icons) from its own server. No third-party server is contacted, no data is sent while you play, and the game works offline.

4. Third-party services

This version uses no advertising, analytics or tracking services. If any are added later, this policy will be updated and the game will say so.

5. Children's privacy

The game is a horror title intended for players aged 13 and over. We knowingly collect no data from children — or from anyone else.

6. Your rights

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing to delete or export. You can clear the settings stored on your device from the in-game settings menu or by clearing your browser data.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated. Significant changes will also be announced in the game.
